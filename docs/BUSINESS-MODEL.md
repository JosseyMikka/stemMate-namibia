# STEMMate sustainability proposal

Status: planning only. There is no implemented checkout, subscription billing, paid access control or revenue in the app. No commercial agreement or price is established by this proposal.

## What customers would pay for

STEMMate needs to offer useful subject/curriculum context, reliable reasoning support, accessible visuals, usable teacher drafts and accountable privacy practices. A generic chatbot alone is difficult to distinguish. Sell dependable educational support and implementation, not a claim that AI replaces teachers or guarantees marks.

Proposed initial buyer: a school, tutoring organisation, NGO or education sponsor. Teachers and learners use the tool; the organisation funds an agreed level of access and support. Individual teacher subscriptions are a later hypothesis, not a prerequisite to testing with schools.

| Route | Proposed paid value | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School/organisation subscription | Teacher access and an agreed pool of learner/AI usage, support and reviewed resources as those are actually delivered | Do not advertise complete curriculum coverage, enterprise SSO or SLAs that do not exist |
| Onboarding/training | Setup help, teacher workshops and responsible classroom-use support | Charge separately for actual services; do not present this as research consent |
| Sponsored learner access | NGOs/businesses fund access for a defined group | No learner-identifying reports to sponsors; sponsorship is not consent for research/data disclosure |
| Optional individual teacher plan | Personal planning and bounded AI usage without class setup | Validate demand first; no-class preparation is implemented, but paid plans are not |

Maintain a useful basic learner route and essential reasoning/accessibility support. Avoid advertising to minors, sales of learner data, punitive streaks and pressure to pay to finish an independent check. Do not rely on a provider's free tier for long-term affordability.

## Unit economics before pricing

Measure actual text and vision requests/tokens, retries, storage, hosting, support hours, content review, training delivery and any payment-processing costs. Quizzes, transcription and long planning drafts have different costs. The current hourly request caps are pilot safeguards, not a subscription/billing meter; quotas reset on API restart and require one worker. They do not establish production capacity or exact provider charges.

Use:

- Monthly revenue = active paying organisations × average monthly fee + delivered service fees.
- Contribution per organisation = fee minus directly attributable AI/hosting/payment/support/content costs.
- Sustainable scale requires total contribution to cover product development, administration and other fixed costs.

Include founder/teacher review time rather than treating it as free. Price only after testing school budgets and usage. No competitor price or promised margin is assumed here.

## Practical validation sequence

1. Speak with several teachers and the person who actually approves school spending. Test the need, alternatives, procurement route and willingness to pay without collecting learner personal records.
2. Run a small, supervised pilot only after the privacy/release gates are met. Record teacher-reported preparation time saved, usefulness/editing burden and actual operating cost. Any research on learner outcomes needs an approved protocol; completion counts are not evidence of learning.
3. Test a written offer with clear scope, bounded usage, support, cancellation and retention terms. Agree sponsored access where affordability is a barrier.
4. Add billing, entitlement enforcement, budget controls and operational support only when demand and delivery capacity are demonstrated. No payment provider has been selected or integrated.
