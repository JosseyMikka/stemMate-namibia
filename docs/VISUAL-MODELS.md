# Explore visually

The chat toolbar opens a secondary panel of original 2D diagrams and three bounded conceptual simulations. Activities are available from independent or class chat, including cross-subject exploration; they do not change the selected chat topic or claim full syllabus alignment. They are not generated by AI and require no additional provider request, iframe, image download or laboratory action.

## Diagrams

- Water, carbon dioxide and methane: element-labelled circles and single/double bond lines show atom connectivity. Drawings do not encode real sizes, colours, bond angles or 3D geometry; methane is explicitly identified as tetrahedral despite its flat schematic.
- Animal cell: membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus and mitochondria, with structure buttons and text explanations. Other organelles are omitted and positions/sizes are schematic. Captions, SVG titles/descriptions and button-based descriptions provide text alternatives.

## Models and scientific limits

| Activity | Model | Assumptions and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Balance atoms | a H₂ + b O₂ → c H₂O, integer coefficients 1–4; exact element counts before/after | Whole-molecule coefficients change, not subscripts. Counts only; no reaction mechanism, energetics, conditions or real experiment. |
| Solute diffusion | Two equal-volume compartments, 24 particles total, four discrete mixing steps. A permeable membrane transfers a rounded quarter of the count difference each step, toward the less populated side. Closed membrane transfers none. | Deterministic net redistribution, not random trajectories or calibrated time/rate. Equal concentration means no net change, not stopped molecular motion. No water/osmosis, charges, pumps or volume changes. |
| Enzyme temperature | Fixed lookup for a fictional enzyme: 5/20/35/50/65 °C → 12/45/100/30/3 relative activity | Illustrative chosen values with an assumed 35 °C optimum, not data or a universal biological curve. pH, enzyme amount and excess substrate fixed. Each trial is a fresh sample; time and irreversible damage are not modelled. No medical or experimental guidance. |

Algorithms and graphics are original. Conceptual references: [PhET balancing equations](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/balancing-chemical-equations), [PhET molecule shapes](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/molecule-shapes), OpenStax Biology 2e [eukaryotic cells](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/4-3-eukaryotic-cells), [passive transport](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/5-2-passive-transport) and [enzymes](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/6-5-enzymes). These links are scientific background, not endorsement or external review of this implementation. Subject-teacher review is required before assessed use. Practical work requires qualified supervision and is outside these activities.

## Reasoning and privacy

1. Choose conditions and write a prediction with a reason. No model result is visible yet.
2. Record the prediction. Conditions lock, then Run model reveals the snapshot result, diagram and table.
3. Record an observation before advancing to explanation.
4. Write an explanation using evidence and a model limitation. Only then can the learner reveal the model explanation.
5. Start another trial, change one condition and predict again. Retain the latest five completed earlier trials for comparison.

This records formative learner self-reports, not grades, scientific reasoning quality or independent attainment. Whitespace is rejected; the app does not judge the prose. Notes are held only in browser memory, never submitted, sent to AI or added to evaluation counts. Activity/view switches and closing preserve simulation notes. New chat, a chat independent check, class/curriculum change, refresh or sign-out clears them; copy any needed notes first.

Explore visually is disabled and its panel unmounted during the chat independent check. Teacher pause disables new model runs and recording. Closing returns keyboard focus to the toolbar. All inputs use native labelled controls; outputs include status text, captions and tables. No motion or timers are required. These remain formative client-side controls, not secure examination restrictions.

## Verification

Unit tests exhaust coefficient combinations, check particle conservation/reflection symmetry/closed membranes/equilibrium, test the illustrative enzyme trend and reject invalid inputs. Workflow tests reject runs before prediction, observations before results and explanations before observation; they verify frozen condition snapshots. Render checks cover SVG accessible alternatives and absence of external images/iframes. Browser tests exercise prediction → observation → explanation, repeat trials, model switching/closing retention, the cell structure controls, both membrane states, enzyme output, 320px reflow and independent-check blocking.
